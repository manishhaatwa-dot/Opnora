AEGIS VOICE

Privacy Policy

Last Updated: November 11, 2026

1. Introduction

Aegis Voice is a personal safety application developed by EKALAVYU STARTUP. The application is designed to provide safety-related features such as voice monitoring, emergency contacts, location access and emergency communication.

This Privacy Policy explains what information the application may access, how it is used and how users can manage their locally stored information.

2. Information and Permissions

Depending on the features enabled by the user, Aegis Voice may request access to the following:

  • Microphone: Used for voice monitoring and voice-trigger-related functionality when enabled.
  • Location: Used to obtain the device's location for emergency location information.
  • SMS: Used to send emergency messages to contacts selected by the user.
  • Phone / Call: Used for emergency calling functionality.
  • Phone State: May be requested for phone-related functionality.

Permissions are requested according to the features being used. Users can manage permissions through Android settings.

3. Emergency Contacts

Users can save emergency contact names and phone numbers within the application.

These contacts are stored locally on the device and are used for emergency communication initiated through the app.

When an emergency SMS is sent, the message and any included location information are shared with the selected recipients through the device's SMS service.

4. Location Information

If location permission is granted, Aegis Voice may access the device's location to provide emergency location information.

Location information may be displayed in the application and included in an emergency message when the user initiates that action.

The application may maintain a local cached location for relevant app functionality. Users can delete this cached information through the Data Deletion feature.

5. Microphone and Voice Data

The microphone may be used when voice monitoring is enabled. Voice-related processing depends on the application's implemented features and permissions.

Saved voice trigger phrases and associated recordings, where created by the application, may be stored locally on the device.

Users can remove supported saved voice phrases and associated recordings using the Data Deletion feature.

6. Data Storage and Security

Aegis Voice stores certain application information locally on the user's device, including saved emergency contacts, voice-related information and cached location data.

Users should protect their devices with appropriate screen locks and device security settings.

No application can guarantee absolute security of data stored or transmitted through a device or external service.

7. Data Sharing

Aegis Voice does not sell users' personal information.

Emergency information may be shared with recipients selected by the user when an emergency SMS or call functionality is used.

SMS and telephone communication are handled through the device's Android services and mobile network provider. Their handling of information is subject to their own policies and practices.

8. Data Deletion

Users can access the Data Deletion option from the Aegis Voice application.

The feature is designed to remove supported locally stored application data, including:

  • Saved emergency contacts
  • Saved voice trigger phrases
  • Associated voice recordings covered by the deletion feature
  • Cached GPS location
  • Local copied voice model file

This action does not remove SMS messages already sent, call history, Android permissions or information stored outside the application's local storage.

More information is available on our Data Deletion page .

9. Children's Privacy

Aegis Voice is not specifically designed to collect personal information from children. Parents or guardians should supervise children's use of the application.

10. Changes to This Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated when application features or privacy practices change. The latest version will be published on this page.

Users are encouraged to review this page periodically.
