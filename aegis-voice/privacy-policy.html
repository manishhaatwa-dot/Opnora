1. Introduction

Aegis Voice is a voice-activated emergency safety application developed and operated by EKALAVYU STARTUP. This Privacy Policy explains how the Aegis Voice mobile application ("the App", "we", "us") handles information when you use its safety, monitoring, and emergency features.

By using Aegis Voice, you agree to the practices described in this Privacy Policy. If you do not agree, please do not use the App.

2. Information and Permissions Used

Aegis Voice requests Android permissions only when they are required for a specific safety or emergency feature. The App does not collect, upload, or store your personal data on a developer-operated server.

Permission Purpose
RECORD_AUDIO Used for Secret Word voice monitoring and voice-trigger detection while monitoring is active.
ACCESS_COARSE_LOCATION Used to obtain an approximate device location for emergency location features.
ACCESS_FINE_LOCATION Used to obtain a precise device location for emergency location features and location links.
ACCESS_BACKGROUND_LOCATION Used when emergency monitoring is active and the App needs location access while running in the background.
FOREGROUND_SERVICE_MICROPHONE Enables the foreground voice-monitoring service to use the microphone while monitoring is active.
FOREGROUND_SERVICE_LOCATION Enables the foreground service to access location-related emergency functionality.
SEND_SMS Used to send emergency SOS messages to your saved emergency contacts, manually or automatically after an emergency trigger.
CALL_PHONE Used to place an emergency call to your first saved emergency contact.
READ_PHONE_STATE Used to check SIM and calling-account availability for emergency call and SMS features.
POST_NOTIFICATIONS Used to show monitoring status, foreground-service activity, and important emergency updates.

3. Microphone and Voice Monitoring

When you turn monitoring ON, Aegis Voice uses your device microphone to listen for a Secret Word that you have configured in the App.

  • Monitoring runs through an Android foreground service.
  • While monitoring is active, Android displays a persistent notification so you can see that the service is running.
  • The purpose of background monitoring is Secret Word detection, not continuous audio recording.
  • Secret Word detection is performed through the App's voice-trigger functionality.
  • Background microphone audio is not uploaded to the developer's server.

Monitoring must remain ON for Secret Word detection to work. If monitoring is stopped, or Android or your device restricts or stops the service, Secret Word detection will not work during that time.

4. Custom Voice Phrases / Samples

You may create and save custom Secret Words in the App. Depending on the feature you use, the App may store voice-trigger settings and associated voice samples locally on your device.

  • Saved Secret Words and associated recordings are stored in the App's private local storage.
  • These recordings are not uploaded to a developer-operated server.
  • You can edit or delete saved Secret Words and recordings from the Secret Word Manager.

5. Automatic Emergency Response

When a configured Secret Word is detected while monitoring is active, Aegis Voice may start an automatic emergency response without requiring you to touch the phone.

Automatic SMS

  • The App attempts to send an emergency SMS to your saved emergency contacts automatically.
  • You do not need to press a Send button for this automatic SMS.
  • The SMS may include an emergency alert, the detected wake phrase, a help message, and an available Google Maps location link.
  • If a fresh location is not available, the App may use the last-known or locally cached location.
  • If no location is available, the App may still attempt to send the SMS without a map link.
  • SMS is submitted through Android's SMS/telephony service and your mobile network operator.
  • SMS delivery is not guaranteed and may depend on SIM availability, network coverage, and operator restrictions.

Automatic Call

  • After the SMS submission step, the App may attempt to place an automatic call to your first valid saved emergency contact.
  • The current implementation checks SIM slot 1 for calling availability.
  • Call success depends on call permission, an active SIM, network coverage, and Android/device restrictions.

Emergency SMS and calls use your SIM and mobile network. Network coverage, SIM availability, operator restrictions, and device settings can affect delivery or connection. SMS or call charges may apply.

6. Manual SOS and Calling

Aegis Voice also provides manual emergency options for situations where you can operate the phone but may not be able to speak or type.

Manual SOS

  • The manual SOS feature attempts to send an emergency SMS to your saved emergency contacts.
  • Manual SOS is separate from the automatic Secret Word response.
  • You initiate manual SOS yourself from the App.

Manual Emergency Call

  • The App provides an option to manually call your first saved emergency contact.
  • You initiate this call yourself from the App.

7. Location and Background Location

Aegis Voice may access your device location for safety and emergency features.

  • Approximate location may be used through coarse location access.
  • Precise location may be used through fine location access.
  • Background location may be used when emergency monitoring is active and the App needs location access while running in the background.
  • Location may be included as a Google Maps link in an emergency SMS sent to your saved emergency contacts.
  • If a fresh location is unavailable, a locally cached last-known location may be used.

Location availability depends on Android location services, GPS settings, permissions, and device conditions.

8. Local Data Storage

Aegis Voice stores the following data locally on your device:

  • Emergency contacts, saved in the App's local SharedPreferences.
  • SOS runtime status, stored in local SharedPreferences.
  • Location fallback data, stored through a local LocationCache.
  • Voice-trigger settings and resources, stored in the App's local storage.

This data remains on your device and is not uploaded to a developer-operated server.

9. Developer Data Collection

EKALAVYU STARTUP does not operate a backend server or cloud database for Aegis Voice user data.

  • The App does not use Firebase.
  • The App does not include Firebase SDK.
  • The App does not use advertising SDKs.
  • The App does not use analytics SDKs.
  • The developer does not have a live-location dashboard for users.
  • The developer does not have a cloud database of emergency contacts.
  • Emergency SMS content and background microphone recordings are not uploaded to the developer's server.

This Policy does not claim that no data ever leaves your device. Emergency SMS and calls travel through your mobile network, and a location link may be shared with your emergency contacts.

10. Sharing with Emergency Contacts and System Services

When an emergency SMS is sent, the following information may be shared with your saved emergency contacts:

  • An emergency alert message.
  • The detected wake phrase, when applicable.
  • A help message.
  • An available Google Maps location link, when location is available.

The App also relies on Android and third-party system services to function:

  • Android location services provide location information.
  • Android telephony services handle SMS and calls.
  • Your mobile network operator processes SMS and calls.
  • Google Maps or a compatible maps app may open a location link.
  • If Android or device speech recognition is used in any feature, that service's own privacy policy may apply.

These are Android operating system services and telecom/network services, not third-party SDKs integrated into the App.

11. Android Backup

If Android backup is enabled on your device, App data may be included in your device backup according to your Android backup settings. You can manage or disable backup through your device's Android settings.

12. Data Retention and Deletion

You can manage and delete your local Aegis Voice data through the App:

  • Emergency contacts can be edited or deleted from the App.
  • Supported voice-trigger data can be deleted through the App's available controls.
  • The App provides a local data deletion function.
  • Clearing App storage from Android Settings or uninstalling the App may also remove local App data.
  • Data included in a device backup is subject to your Android backup settings.
  • SMS messages already sent and telecom operator records cannot be deleted by the App.
  • Because the developer does not operate a user-data database, the developer does not have a separate data-deletion database.

13. Security

Aegis Voice is designed to keep your safety data on your device. The App uses Android's standard local storage mechanisms for contacts, SOS status, location cache, and voice-trigger data.

No security measure can guarantee complete protection. You should use a strong device lock, keep your Android system updated, and review the permissions granted to the App.

14. User Choices and Permissions

You can control how Aegis Voice works through Android permissions and App settings:

  • You can grant or deny Android permissions at any time through device settings.
  • You can turn monitoring ON or OFF from the App.
  • You can add, edit, or delete emergency contacts.
  • You can add, edit, or delete Secret Words and recordings.
  • You can delete local App data through the Data Deletion feature.
  • You can uninstall the App to remove its local data, subject to Android backup settings.
