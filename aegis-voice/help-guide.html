AEGIS VOICE • USER GUIDE

Welcome to Aegis Voice

Aegis Voice brings together voice-trigger settings, emergency contacts, GPS location and SOS messaging in one app. Read this guide before relying on the app in an emergency.

Keep your phone charged, maintain mobile network access, and make sure your emergency contacts are current.

01 · Voice Trigger Manager

  1. Open Voice Trigger from the dashboard.
  2. Enter your secret phrase and tap Record Phrase.
  3. Use Play Recording to listen.
  4. Tap Test Voice Phrase to test the phrase.
  5. Tap Save Phrase to save it.
  6. Manage saved phrases using Edit, Play or Delete.

Choose a phrase that is easy to remember and keep it private.

02 · Start and Stop Monitoring

Tap START MONITORING. Allow Android permissions requested by the app, including microphone and location. Background location access may need to be enabled separately in Android settings.

When monitoring is running, the dashboard displays its status. Tap STOP MONITORING to stop the monitoring service.

03 · Emergency Contacts

  • Enter the contact name and mobile number.
  • Tap SAVE CONTACT.
  • Use EDIT to update a contact.
  • Use DELETE to remove a contact.

Check every number carefully and keep trusted contacts informed.

04 · GPS Location

Tap GET MY LOCATION to request your current location. The app displays latitude and longitude when available.

Tap OPEN IN GOOGLE MAPS to view the location in Maps.

Location availability depends on phone settings, permissions, GPS reception and network conditions.

05 · Emergency Call

CALL TO FIRST CONTACT starts a call to the first saved emergency contact. Allow phone-call permission when Android asks.

Place the contact you want to reach first at the top of your saved contacts list.

06 · SOS Emergency SMS

Tap PREVIEW SOS MESSAGE to review the message. Previewing does not send an SMS.

Tap SEND SOS SMS when you intend to send the alert to your saved contacts. Allow SMS permission when requested.

The message includes a location link when a location is available. SMS submission does not guarantee delivery; delivery depends on the SIM, operator, signal and recipient availability.

07 · SIM and Phone Settings

Keep an active SIM in your phone and ensure calling and SMS services are available.

If your phone has multiple SIMs, review Android's default SMS and calling SIM settings. Network coverage and SIM availability can affect emergency communication.

08 · Troubleshooting

Monitoring does not start:

  • Check microphone and location permissions.
  • Review background location settings.
  • Keep phone location enabled.

GPS is unavailable:

  • Turn on phone location.
  • Check app location permission.
  • Try GET MY LOCATION again.

SMS or calls do not work:

  • Check the relevant app permission.
  • Confirm saved phone numbers.
  • Check SIM service and network signal.

09 · Privacy and Data Deletion

Use the app's Privacy Policy link to review privacy information.

Use Data Deletion in the app to review and delete supported local app data.

Deleting local app data does not delete SMS messages already sent, call history held by Android or your operator, or data stored outside the app.

Important Safety Notice

Aegis Voice is a safety-support tool. It is not a replacement for emergency services, direct help, or contacting local authorities. Do not assume an alert, call or SMS has been delivered until you have confirmation. In an immediate emergency, contact the appropriate local emergency service directly.
